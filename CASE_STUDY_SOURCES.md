This website contains original, newly generated charts and conceptual diagrams. No employer source code, screenshots, customer names, rates, internal KPIs, credentials or datasets are reproduced. Illustrations are labeled when synthetic or reconstructed. Before publication, review client/project naming and obtain necessary permissions for any brand references or previously unpublished details.

## Academic evidence

- **Retail optimization:** `Proyecto Coppel.pdf`, 1-page team academic poster. It documents Dynamic Time Warping, five forecast models per cluster, Random Forest, forecast index, Python/Gurobi and scenario figures 94M / 122.5M / 135.8M with +51% reported improvement. These are modeled outcomes, not audited realized profit.
- **MiBici:** `A01641407_Etapa 3_MiBici (1).pdf`, 65-page report. Nearest-neighbor, convex-hull, capacity and fleet scenarios. Distances/times for 3/5/7 trucks as documented. Its conclusion contains an apparent duplicated phrase about minimizing distance; we report the numerical trade-off instead.
- **Scheduling:** `Proyecto Optimizacion python.pdf`, 5-page academic team report. Contains gurobipy model, two machines, eight jobs, makespan and scheduling constraints; no verified optimum is claimed.
- **Lean:** `Proyecto Scapinno.pdf`, 58-page team report. Layout travel 216.8 to 166m; layout table 25 to 28 cuts; later proposed combined scenario 36 to 63 cuts. Different scopes, not combined into a single impact claim. Kaizen table credits Santiago with spaghetti diagram.
- **Industrial downtime:** `Reporte Flex.docx`, 40-page academic team report. SIPOC, PDCA, FMEA, Ishikawa, Kruskal-Wallis, Mann-Whitney, Pareto, control charts. No confirmed numerical downtime reduction.
- **Economic evaluation:** `Reporte Economia.pdf`, 7-page academic team report. Bakery assumptions 4,000 daily demand and 5,000 daily capacity.
